故障排查

Troubleshooting

按顺序尝试以下步骤，多数问题都可以自行解决。

Try the steps in order — most issues can be resolved on your own.

授权后 App 内步数仍为 0
Steps still show 0 after granting access
  1. 打开系统「健康」App，确认「步数」下有今日数据
  2. Open the Health app and confirm today's step count exists
  3. 在「设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 健康 > StepJoy」确认步数与距离开关已打开
  4. In Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > StepJoy, make sure Step Count and Distance are both on
  5. 完全退出并重新打开 StepJoy
  6. Quit and reopen StepJoy
  7. 若仍为 0，在「健康」App 的数据来源中确认 iPhone 计步正常
  8. If it's still 0, check the Health app's data sources to confirm step counting works on your iPhone
线路进度与预期不符
Route progress doesn't look right
  1. 确认顶部当前选中的线路，进度只累加到当前线路
  2. Check which route is selected at the top — progress only accumulates on the current route
  3. 确认数据起点：StepJoy 以安装 App 之后的运动数据计算
  4. Confirm the starting point: StepJoy counts activity accumulated after the App was installed
  5. 切换线路后，展示的是该线路自己的独立进度，属正常现象
  6. After switching routes, you see that route's own independent progress — this is expected
App 闪退或异常
The App crashes or misbehaves
  1. 在 App Store 更新到最新版本
  2. Update to the latest version on the App Store
  3. 重启 iPhone 后重试
  4. Restart your iPhone and try again
  5. 卸载重装（注意：会清除本地进度数据）
  6. Uninstall and reinstall (note: this erases local progress)
  7. 仍无法解决时，联系我们并提供截图与版本号
  8. If it persists, contact us with a screenshot and the version number
