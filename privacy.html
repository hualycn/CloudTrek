引言

Introduction

本隐私政策（下称「本政策」）说明 StepJoy（下称「本应用」或「我们」）如何收集、使用、存储和保护你的信息。请在使用本应用前完整阅读本政策。

StepJoy 是一款运动数据可视化应用：在获得你的授权后，读取 Apple 健康（HealthKit）中的步数与步行 / 跑步距离，将累计运动数据映射到内置线路上，把每天的走路变成一场「云旅行」。

本应用是单机应用：无账号体系、无后端服务器、无任何网络上报。我们同时遵守适用的数据保护法律（包括《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》）以及 Apple 关于健康数据使用的相关要求。

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") explains how StepJoy (the "App", "we", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. Please read this Policy in full before using the App.

StepJoy is an activity data visualization app. With your authorization, it reads step count and walking + running distance from Apple Health (HealthKit) and maps your accumulated activity onto built-in routes, turning each day of walking into a "virtual journey".

The App runs entirely on-device: no account system, no backend server, and no network transmission. We also comply with applicable data protection laws (including the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China) and Apple's requirements regarding health data.

01

我们收集的信息

Information We Collect

健康数据（经你授权读取）。本应用仅申请对以下两类健康数据的读取权限，不写入、不修改、不删除你的任何健康数据：

  • 步数（Step Count）；
  • 步行 / 跑步距离（Walking + Running Distance）。

上述数据直接由 Apple 健康（HealthKit）提供，本应用不会在设备之外另行保存原始健康样本。

本地使用数据。你在各条线路上累计的步数、距离与进度。这些数据仅保存在你的设备本地，用于计算和展示线路进度。

我们不收集的信息。本应用不要求注册，不收集你的姓名、手机号、电子邮箱、通讯录、照片、精确定位、广告标识符（IDFA）等个人信息；不采集设备标识或任何可用于识别你的信息；不嵌入第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK。

Health data (read with your authorization). The App only requests read access to the following two types of health data. It never writes to, modifies, or deletes any of your health data:

  • Step count;
  • Walking + running distance.

This data comes directly from Apple Health (HealthKit). The App does not store raw health samples outside your device.

Usage data generated locally. Your accumulated steps, distance, and progress on each route. This data is stored only on your device and is used solely to calculate and display route progress.

What we do not collect. The App requires no registration and does not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers (IDFA). It does not collect device identifiers or any identifying information, and it does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

02

我们如何使用信息

How We Use Information

我们仅将上述信息用于以下目的：

  • 以你首次使用本应用的时间为起点，读取并累计此后的步数与距离，计算你在线路上的位置、节点进度与整体进度；
  • 在应用内展示当天及累计的步数与距离；
  • 优化线路进度计算与本地体验。

全部计算均在你的设备本地完成，健康数据不会离开你的设备。我们不会将你的信息用于任何其他目的，包括广告投放、用户画像或第三方营销。

We use the information described above only for the following purposes:

  • Using the time you first launched the App as the starting point, reading and accumulating steps and distance since then to calculate your position, node progress, and overall progress along routes;
  • Displaying your current-day and cumulative steps and distance within the App;
  • Improving route progress calculation and the local experience.

All calculations are performed locally on your device, and health data never leaves your device. We do not use your information for any other purpose, including advertising, profiling, or third-party marketing.

03

信息的存储与保护

Storage and Security

健康数据。由 Apple 健康（HealthKit）在你设备上保管。本应用仅按你的授权读取，不将原始健康样本复制到本应用的存储中；本地仅保存由步数 / 距离换算得到的线路进度数值。

应用数据。线路、节点、进度与应用状态使用 Apple SwiftData 持久化存储于本应用在 iOS 中的本地沙盒，受 iOS 系统安全机制保护。

传输安全。本应用不向任何服务器传输数据，因此不存在数据在传输过程中被第三方接触的场景。

Health data. Stored by Apple Health (HealthKit) on your device. The App reads it only under your authorization and does not copy raw health samples into its own storage; locally, it keeps only route progress figures derived from steps and distance.

App data. Routes, nodes, progress, and app state are persisted locally using Apple SwiftData in the App's iOS sandbox, protected by iOS system security.

Transmission security. The App does not transmit any data to servers, so no third party can access your data during transmission.

05

你的权利与选择

Your Rights and Choices

撤销健康数据授权。你可以在 iOS「设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 健康」中随时查看并撤销本应用对健康数据的访问权限。撤销后，本应用将无法读取新的健康数据。

删除本地数据。卸载本应用，或在「设置 > 通用 > iPhone 储存空间」中删除本应用及其数据，即可清除全部本地进度数据。

查询与更正。应用内展示的数据均来源于你设备上的 Apple 健康数据与本地进度，你可以直接在 Apple 健康中查看和管理原始健康数据。

联系我们。如你对个人信息处理有任何疑问或请求，可通过文末联系方式与我们联系，我们将在合理期限内处理。

Revoke health data access. You can review and revoke the App's access to health data at any time under iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health. After revocation, the App can no longer read new health data.

Delete local data. Uninstall the App, or delete the App and its data under Settings > General > iPhone Storage, to erase all local progress data.

Review and correct. Data displayed in the App comes from Apple Health data and local progress on your device; you can view and manage the underlying health data directly in Apple Health.

Contact us. If you have any questions or requests regarding the processing of your information, contact us via the details at the end of this Policy and we will respond within a reasonable period.
